# Character Proportion


The character should look like a cute collectible RPG character.


Body ratio:


Head:

36%


Body:

44%


Legs:

20%



Characteristics:


- slightly oversized head
- small cute body
- short legs
- tiny hands and feet
- rounded silhouette


The character should feel like:

a cute game avatar,
not a realistic miniature human.


Avoid:

- realistic human proportions
- long legs
- tall body
